How They Actually Produce It

Script rules

  • Title sells identity upgrade, not a lecture
  • Hook names the private behavior in the first 10–25 seconds
  • Treat the flaw as an adaptive strategy, then flip it into a rare advantage
  • 2–4 named research beats, spoken in plain language
  • 3–5 concrete rules at the end
  • Calm adult voice, short sentences, second person

Visual & editing

  • 200–350 stills for a 12–16 minute video
  • One still per short beat (roughly every 2–5 seconds)
  • Light motion only: slow 102–105% scale or gentle drift
  • Frequent cuts timed to the voiceover
  • Almost no text on the image (max 4–5 words if any)
  • Captions: large, one short clause at a time
  • Music very low or none

Workflow order

  1. Pick one trait or private behavior
  2. Write the title as a status reversal
  3. Write the hook before the research
  4. Add 2–4 real studies
  5. Write 3–5 named rules
  6. Break into 200+ beats
  7. Generate every frame from the same locked prompt
  8. Calm voiceover
  9. Edit with light motion + frequent cuts
